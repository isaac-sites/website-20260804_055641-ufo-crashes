## Introduction

Professional [witnesses]({{ 'witnesses/' | relative_url }}) are often treated as the strongest eyewitnesses in alleged UFO crash cases. Pilots, police officers, military personnel and air traffic professionals usually have more experience observing aircraft, judging flight behaviour and reporting unusual events than the general public. That experience genuinely improves the quality of many observations. However, it does not make them immune to the limitations of human perception, particularly at night. Aviation training itself emphasises that darkness degrades depth perception, obscures the horizon and creates well-documented visual illusions capable of misleading even experienced crews.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aopa.org/training-and-safety/online-learning/safety-spotlights/spatial-disorientation/tricked-by-illusions" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aopa.org">[AOPA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aopa.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Tricked by Illusions</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Tricked by Illusions - AOPA...</span></span></span>

For investigators examining reports of [UFO crashes]({{ 'ufo-crashes/' | relative_url }}), the practical lesson is straightforward: professional credentials should increase confidence that a witness attempted a careful observation, but they should never substitute for analysing viewing conditions. A highly trained observer can provide valuable evidence while still misjudging an object's distance, speed or apparent descent because the visual information available was fundamentally ambiguous.

## What professional training genuinely adds

Professional observers differ from casual witnesses in several important ways.

Pilots and military aircrew are trained to recognise conventional aircraft lighting, understand flight profiles, estimate bearings and communicate observations using standard terminology. Police officers often receive training in systematic incident reporting and are accustomed to documenting events shortly after they occur. Military personnel may also be familiar with sensors, radar procedures and the importance of distinguishing observation from interpretation.

These skills provide several advantages:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* More consistent descriptions of direction, altitude and timing.
* Better recognition of known aircraft lighting configurations.
* Greater awareness of weather, visibility and aviation activity.
* More disciplined reporting compared with informal recollections.

</div>

Research on eyewitness expertise generally finds that domain knowledge improves recognition of familiar patterns but does not eliminate perceptual error when the available visual information is poor. Expertise helps identify known objects more accurately; it cannot recover information that never reached the observer's eyes in the first place.

This distinction is particularly important in UFO [crash narratives]({{ 'crash-narrative/' | relative_url }}). A pilot may reliably report seeing an unusual light disappear beyond a ridge. The conclusion that the object struck the ground is a separate inference requiring evidence beyond the observation itself.

## Which night-time illusions expertise cannot remove

Night flying manuals devote extensive attention to visual illusions precisely because experienced pilots remain vulnerable to them.

### Distance and size become inseparable

Darkness removes many of the visual references that normally allow people to judge range. Without terrain detail, shadows or familiar objects, a small nearby light and a much larger distant light can appear nearly identical.

This uncertainty affects every subsequent estimate. If the observer unconsciously assumes the object is closer than it really is, it will also appear smaller, slower and lower. If the assumed distance is too great, the inferred object becomes correspondingly larger and faster.

Professional training cannot recreate missing depth cues.

### Apparent motion may not be real motion

One of the best-known night-time visual illusions is autokinesis. When someone stares at a small stationary light against a dark background for several seconds, the light can appear to drift or manoeuvre even though it remains fixed. Aviation safety guidance warns pilots specifically about this illusion because it can lead them to mistake stars or stationary lights for moving aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aopa.org/training-and-safety/online-learning/safety-spotlights/spatial-disorientation/tricked-by-illusions" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aopa.org">[AOPA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aopa.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Tricked by Illusions</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Tricked by Illusions - AOPA...</span></span></span>

Similarly, an observer moving in a vehicle or aircraft can perceive dramatic object motion through parallax. The apparent movement arises because the observer is changing position relative to the background rather than because the observed object is accelerating.

The US Department of Defense's All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) has highlighted this principle in its analysis of several military UAP cases. Its technical reports explain that motion parallax and forced perspective can substantially distort estimates of speed, direction and distance when observations come from a rapidly moving platform or a single viewing position.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/Information%20Papers/AARO_Effect_of_Forced_Perspective_and_Parallax_View_on_UAP_Observations_2024.pdf?ver=WTwJtQaCvC3BkRlBJCGLzg%3D%3D" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aaro.mil">[AARO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aaro.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Effect of Forced Perspective and Parallax View on UAP Observations</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Effect of Forced Perspective and Parallax View on UAP ObservationsMay 8, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 8, 2024</span></span></span>

### The horizon can disappear

Night removes another critical reference: the visible horizon.

Aviation training repeatedly warns that over dark terrain or water, pilots may lose their normal sense of orientation. Ground lights may be mistaken for stars, stars for aircraft and featureless darkness for open sky. These illusions are sufficiently common that they are standard components of pilot safety education rather than rare psychological curiosities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aopa.org/training-and-safety/online-learning/safety-spotlights/spatial-disorientation/tricked-by-illusions" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aopa.org">[AOPA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aopa.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Tricked by Illusions</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Tricked by Illusions - AOPA...</span></span></span>

The same visual limitations apply when experienced observers watch unusual lights from the ground.

## Military experience does not prevent ambiguous sightings

Some of the most widely discussed [unidentified]({{ 'unidentified/' | relative_url }}) aerial phenomena have involved military personnel, leading some commentators to argue that professional expertise alone establishes exceptional reliability.

A more cautious interpretation is supported by official investigations.

The [AARO review]({{ 'aaro-review/' | relative_url }}) of the Navy's "GoFast" infrared video concluded that the object's apparently extraordinary speed resulted primarily from viewing geometry and motion parallax rather than anomalous acceleration. Importantly, the office did not dismiss the observers as dishonest or incompetent. Instead, it demonstrated that experienced aircrew could observe an event accurately while naturally interpreting ambiguous visual information in ways later corrected through geometric reconstruction.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/Information%20Papers/AARO_Effect_of_Forced_Perspective_and_Parallax_View_on_UAP_Observations_2024.pdf?ver=WTwJtQaCvC3BkRlBJCGLzg%3D%3D" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aaro.mil">[AARO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aaro.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Effect of Forced Perspective and Parallax View on UAP Observations</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Effect of Forced Perspective and Parallax View on UAP ObservationsMay 8, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 8, 2024</span></span></span>

This distinction matters. A trained observer may correctly report:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* when the observation occurred;
* where they were located;
* the object's apparent bearing;
* how long it remained visible.

</div>

The interpretation of altitude, size, speed or whether an object actually descended to the ground often requires independent reconstruction using geometry, sensor data or multiple observation points.

## Why confidence and accuracy are different

One of the more surprising findings from eyewitness research is that confidence and accuracy are only weakly related once conditions become difficult.

Professional witnesses frequently appear credible because they communicate clearly, use technical vocabulary and genuinely believe their observations. None of these characteristics guarantees that their perception was accurate.

Stress can further complicate matters. Unexpected aerial events naturally attract intense attention, narrowing focus onto the unusual object while reducing awareness of surrounding reference cues. Later recollection reconstructs the event using memory, prior knowledge and subsequent discussion rather than replaying a perfect recording.

Professional experience may improve the structure of a report without preventing these normal cognitive processes.

## How investigators should weigh credentials against conditions

Experienced investigators generally treat witness expertise as one factor among many rather than a substitute for physical evidence.

A balanced assessment asks several questions:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--metric" markdown="1">

* **What exactly did the witness observe?** Separate direct observation from later interpretation.
* **What were the viewing conditions?** Darkness, haze, moon phase and background lighting often matter more than professional status.
* **Was the witness moving?** Observations from aircraft or vehicles increase the possibility of parallax and relative-motion errors.
* **Were there independent observers?** Agreement from multiple separated locations provides stronger evidence than repeated accounts from a single viewpoint.
* **Is there corroborating data?** Radar, photographs with metadata, astronomical information, aircraft records or weather observations can test interpretations that unaided vision cannot.

</div>

Professional credentials should therefore increase the weight given to careful reporting, not eliminate the need for corroboration.

## What this means for alleged UFO crash reports

Within UFO crash investigations, reports from pilots, police officers and military personnel deserve careful attention because they often provide disciplined observations recorded with useful operational detail. The strongest investigations neither dismiss expert witnesses nor treat them as infallible. Instead, they recognise that the reliability of any night-time UFO observation depends less on who saw it than on what information the viewing conditions actually made available. Professional training improves observation, but it cannot transform fundamentally ambiguous visual scenes into precise measurements of an unknown object's distance, motion or fate.

    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
