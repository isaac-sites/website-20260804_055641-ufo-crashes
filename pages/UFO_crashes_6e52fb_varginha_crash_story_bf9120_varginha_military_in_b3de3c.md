## Introduction

The formal Brazilian military inquiry into the Varginha case was not an investigation into whether extraterrestrial life existed. Its specific purpose was much narrower: to determine whether Brazilian Army personnel had secretly captured and transported a non-human being, as alleged in books, media reports and UFO investigations. After reviewing military records, interviewing named personnel and examining the movements of vehicles said to have been involved, the inquiry concluded that it found no documentary or testimonial evidence supporting a military recovery operation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://noticias.uol.com.br/ultimas-noticias/agencia-estado/2026/01/06/stm-divulga-dados-de-inquerito-sobre-et-de-varginha-caso-fruto-de-engano-e-boatos.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noticias.uol.com.br">[UOL Notcias]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noticias.uol.com.br</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UOL NotciasSTM divulga dados de inqurito sobre ET de Varginha: &#x27;caso fruto de engano e boatos&#x27;January 6, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 6, 2026</span></span></span>

The inquiry therefore focused on whether the alleged military operation left an administrative trail rather than on broader questions about [unidentified]({{ 'unidentified/' | relative_url }}) phenomena.

## Why the military inquiry was opened

The inquiry, an *Inqurito Policial Militar* (Military Police Inquiry, or IPM), was opened after allegations that soldiers from the Army's Escola de Sargentos das Armas (School of Sergeants of the Arms) had captured and transported an unusual creature in January 1996. The published allegations named specific officers, drivers, trucks and routes rather than making vague claims, allowing investigators to compare the stories against official records.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-br-varginha-ipm-1996-exercito-varginha-ipm-volume01-1996" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl br varginha ipm 1996 exercito varginha ipm volume01 1996</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyCaso Varginha, IPM (Inqurito Policial Militar) Volume 01 (1996), Fora Area Brasileira / Arquivo Nacional 1996 UFO...</span></span></span>

The investigation assembled witness testimony from military personnel, examined internal administrative documents and reviewed vehicle movement records. Because the allegations centred on identifiable Army units and equipment, the inquiry was able to test concrete factual claims rather than relying solely on recollections or rumours.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-br-varginha-ipm-1996-exercito-varginha-ipm-volume01-1996" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl br varginha ipm 1996 exercito varginha ipm volume01 1996</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyCaso Varginha, IPM (Inqurito Policial Militar) Volume 01 (1996), Fora Area Brasileira / Arquivo Nacional 1996 UFO...</span></span></span>

## Did vehicle records match the alleged recovery?

One of the inquiry's principal tests concerned the [military vehicles]({{ 'recovery-vehicles/' | relative_url }}) said to have transported the creature.

According to the allegations, Army trucks carried a recovered being between locations in and around Varginha before it was transferred elsewhere. Investigators therefore reviewed the documented schedules, maintenance records and recorded movements of the vehicles identified in those accounts. Several of the trucks had verifiable routine duties, including documented journeys associated with servicing at a local Mercedes-Benz dealership, allowing their official movements to be reconstructed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-br-varginha-ipm-1996-exercito-varginha-ipm-volume01-1996" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl br varginha ipm 1996 exercito varginha ipm volume01 1996</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyCaso Varginha, IPM (Inqurito Policial Militar) Volume 01 (1996), Fora Area Brasileira / Arquivo Nacional 1996 UFO...</span></span></span>

The inquiry reported that the recorded routes, timings and administrative documentation did not support the alleged recovery mission. Investigators stated that they found no movements compatible with the transport operation described in UFO literature and no accompanying operational documentation that would normally be expected for such military activity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://noticias.uol.com.br/ultimas-noticias/agencia-estado/2026/01/06/stm-divulga-dados-de-inquerito-sobre-et-de-varginha-caso-fruto-de-engano-e-boatos.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noticias.uol.com.br">[UOL Notcias]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noticias.uol.com.br</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UOL NotciasSTM divulga dados de inqurito sobre ET de Varginha: &#x27;caso fruto de engano e boatos&#x27;January 6, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 6, 2026</span></span></span>

Equally significant was what investigators did **not** find. They reported no orders authorising a recovery mission, no transport manifests, no operational logs describing the capture of an unknown organism and no administrative paperwork indicating an exceptional deployment by the units concerned.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://noticias.uol.com.br/ultimas-noticias/agencia-estado/2026/01/06/stm-divulga-dados-de-inquerito-sobre-et-de-varginha-caso-fruto-de-engano-e-boatos.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noticias.uol.com.br">[UOL Notcias]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noticias.uol.com.br</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UOL NotciasSTM divulga dados de inqurito sobre ET de Varginha: &#x27;caso fruto de engano e boatos&#x27;January 6, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 6, 2026</span></span></span>

## What soldiers and officers told investigators

The inquiry interviewed military personnel whose names had appeared in published accounts of the Varginha incident.

According to the inquiry records, those individuals consistently denied participating in the alleged capture or transport of an extraterrestrial creature. Officers and enlisted personnel disputed the published descriptions of their involvement and rejected claims that Army vehicles had been used in the manner described by UFO investigators.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://noticias.uol.com.br/ultimas-noticias/agencia-estado/2026/01/06/stm-divulga-dados-de-inquerito-sobre-et-de-varginha-caso-fruto-de-engano-e-boatos.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noticias.uol.com.br">[UOL Notcias]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noticias.uol.com.br</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UOL NotciasSTM divulga dados de inqurito sobre ET de Varginha: &#x27;caso fruto de engano e boatos&#x27;January 6, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 6, 2026</span></span></span>

The inquiry also examined the publications that had popularised the recovery narrative and questioned their authors as part of the investigation. ## What the inquiry concludedand what it did not

## The inquiry's strengths and limitations

The inquiry had several notable strengths.

It examined identifiable military units, reviewed official administrative records, reconstructed vehicle movements where documentation existed and interviewed personnel directly named in the allegations. These are precisely the kinds of evidence expected in an investigation of an alleged military operation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-br-varginha-ipm-1996-exercito-varginha-ipm-volume01-1996" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">intl br varginha ipm 1996 exercito varginha ipm volume01 1996</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyCaso Varginha, IPM (Inqurito Policial Militar) Volume 01 (1996), Fora Area Brasileira / Arquivo Nacional 1996 UFO...</span></span></span>

At the same time, the inquiry has recognised limitations.

Its findings depended heavily on the completeness and accuracy of surviving official records and on witness testimony from military personnel. As with many historical investigations into disputed events, the absence of documentary evidence cannot logically prove that an event was impossible; it can only show that investigators found no records supporting it. Supporters of the Varginha recovery narrative have continued to argue that genuinely clandestine operations might leave little or no documentary trace, while critics note that extraordinary claims require positive evidence rather than arguments from missing records.

Within the available documentary record, however, the military inquiry's central finding remains straightforward: investigators checked the personnel, vehicles and administrative records cited in the recovery allegations and reported that they found no evidence confirming that the Brazilian Army captured or transported a non-human being near Varginha.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://noticias.uol.com.br/ultimas-noticias/agencia-estado/2026/01/06/stm-divulga-dados-de-inquerito-sobre-et-de-varginha-caso-fruto-de-engano-e-boatos.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noticias.uol.com.br">[UOL Notcias]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noticias.uol.com.br</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UOL NotciasSTM divulga dados de inqurito sobre ET de Varginha: &#x27;caso fruto de engano e boatos&#x27;January 6, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 6, 2026</span></span></span>
