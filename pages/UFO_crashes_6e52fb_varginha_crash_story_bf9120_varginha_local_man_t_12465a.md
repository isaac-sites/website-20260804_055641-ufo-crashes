## Introduction

One of the central sceptical explanations for the Varginha creature reports is that the three [witnesses]({{ 'witnesses/' | relative_url }}) briefly saw a familiar local resident rather than an unknown being. The Brazilian [military inquiry]({{ 'military-inquiry/' | relative_url }}) argued that weather, poor visibility and fear led the witnesses to misinterpret the appearance of Luís Antônio de Paula, a man with disabilities who was known locally for walking in a crouched posture. This explanation is significant because it attempts to account for the original sighting without invoking an extraterrestrial event. However, it has never been conclusively demonstrated that de Paula was present at the location when the encounter occurred, and the witnesses have consistently maintained that they recognised him as a local resident and that he was not the figure they saw. The result is not a settled identification but a dispute between an official reconstruction and eyewitness testimony.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://noticias.uol.com.br/ultimas-noticias/agencia-estado/2026/01/07/caso-de-et-de-varginha-faz-30-anos-saiba-o-que-investigacao-mostrou.amp.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noticias.uol.com.br">[UOL Notícias]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noticias.uol.com.br</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UOL NotíciasCaso de ET de Varginha faz 30 anos: saiba o que investigação mostrou - 07/01/2026 - UOL NotíciasJanuary 7, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 7, 2026</span></span></span>

## Why investigators identified Luís Antônio de Paula

The official explanation emerged during the Military Police Inquiry (Inquérito Policial Militar, or IPM), which examined allegations that Brazilian military personnel had captured and transported an extraterrestrial creature. Rather than accepting that an unknown entity had been seen, investigators sought a conventional explanation for the initial encounter.

## How weather and perception support the theory

The local man explanation depends less on proving de Paula's presence than on showing how ordinary perception can fail under stressful conditions.

Investigators pointed to reports of severe weather on 20 January 1996, including heavy rain and hail. A person sheltering beside a wall could have appeared muddy, soaked and partially obscured. Reduced visibility, combined with the witnesses' surprise, may have exaggerated unusual features such as posture, body proportions or skin appearance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://noticias.uol.com.br/ultimas-noticias/agencia-estado/2026/01/07/caso-de-et-de-varginha-faz-30-anos-saiba-o-que-investigacao-mostrou.amp.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noticias.uol.com.br">[UOL Notícias]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noticias.uol.com.br</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UOL NotíciasCaso de ET de Varginha faz 30 anos: saiba o que investigação mostrou - 07/01/2026 - UOL NotíciasJanuary 7, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 7, 2026</span></span></span>

Psychological research on eyewitness perception broadly supports the possibility of sincere mistakes under stressful circumstances. Unexpected encounters are particularly vulnerable to errors involving:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* estimating height and body shape;
* interpreting unfamiliar postures;
* remembering colours accurately;
* filling gaps in perception after the event; and
* reinforcing memories through repeated discussion or interviews.

</div>

None of these principles proves that de Paula was the figure seen in Varginha. Rather, they explain why investigators considered mistaken identity a realistic possibility even if the witnesses were reporting honestly.

## Why the witnesses rejected the explanation

The strongest challenge to the local man theory comes from the original witnesses themselves.

Liliane Silva, Valquíria Silva and Kátia Xavier have repeatedly stated that they already knew Luís Antônio de Paula by sight because he was a familiar resident. Their argument is straightforward: if the crouching figure had been de Paula, they would have recognised him immediately instead of fleeing in terror.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://english.elpais.com/international/2026-01-20/the-et-of-varginha-captivates-brazil-30-years-after-sighting-dismissed-by-a-military-investigation.html?outputType=amp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: english.elpais.com">[EL PAÍS English]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">english.elpais.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on elpais.com.</span></span></span>

The women also continued to describe features they believed differed markedly from any human appearance, including:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* unusually large red eyes;
* a disproportionately large head;
* dark brown, shiny skin;
* three rounded protrusions on the head; and
* an appearance they considered unlike any local resident.

</div>

These descriptions remained broadly consistent across later interviews, although details varied somewhat over time, as commonly occurs in eyewitness recollections. Supporters of the Varginha case argue that such consistency weakens the mistaken identity hypothesis, while sceptics counter that consistent memories can still be inaccurate if they originated from a powerful but mistaken first impression.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/fsp/brasil/fc1101200909.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: www1.folha.uol.com.br">[Folha de S.Paulo]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">www1.folha.uol.com.br</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Folha de S.PauloFolha de S.Paulo - O mistério do ET de Varginha - 11/01/2009...</span></span></span>

## What the local man theory does—and does not—establish

The military explanation is often misunderstood as a proven identification. In reality, it is an inference rather than a demonstrated fact.

The inquiry did not establish that:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* de Paula was positively documented at the exact location and time of the sighting;
* the witnesses ever identified him as the figure;
* forensic evidence connected him to the reported encounter; or
* independent physical evidence confirmed the reconstruction.

</div>

Instead, investigators concluded that he represented the most plausible ordinary explanation available from the evidence they examined.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://noticias.uol.com.br/ultimas-noticias/agencia-estado/2026/01/07/caso-de-et-de-varginha-faz-30-anos-saiba-o-que-investigacao-mostrou.amp.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noticias.uol.com.br">[UOL Notícias]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noticias.uol.com.br</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UOL NotíciasCaso de ET de Varginha faz 30 anos: saiba o que investigação mostrou - 07/01/2026 - UOL NotíciasJanuary 7, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 7, 2026</span></span></span>

Likewise, rejecting the identification does not establish that the witnesses encountered an extraterrestrial being. The absence of a confirmed mundane identification is not positive evidence for an extraordinary one. The case therefore remains balanced between two unproven positions: an official hypothesis of mistaken identity and eyewitness insistence that the explanation does not fit.

## How this explanation fits the wider Varginha narrative

The local man theory addresses only the original creature sighting that helped launch the Varginha story. It does not, by itself, explain later claims involving [military vehicles]({{ 'recovery-vehicles/' | relative_url }}), [hospital rumours]({{ 'hospital-rumours/' | relative_url }}) or alleged creature recoveries. Those later elements depend on separate testimony and evidence and cannot be confirmed or rejected solely by identifying the figure seen by the three women.

For that reason, the debate over Luís Antônio de Paula remains one of the most important fault lines in the Varginha case. If investigators were correct, the incident began with a sincere but ordinary misidentification that later accumulated rumours and extraordinary interpretations. If the witnesses were correct that they could not have mistaken a well-known local resident, then the original sighting remains [unexplained]({{ 'unexplained/' | relative_url }})—but still unsupported by independent physical evidence linking it to an extraterrestrial event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://noticias.uol.com.br/ultimas-noticias/agencia-estado/2026/01/07/caso-de-et-de-varginha-faz-30-anos-saiba-o-que-investigacao-mostrou.amp.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noticias.uol.com.br">[com.br]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noticias.uol.com.br</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UOL NotíciasCaso de ET de Varginha faz 30 anos: saiba o que investigação mostrou - 07/01/2026 - UOL NotíciasJanuary 7, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 7, 2026</span></span></span>
