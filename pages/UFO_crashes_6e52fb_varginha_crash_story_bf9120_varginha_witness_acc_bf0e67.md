## Introduction

The three [witnesses]({{ 'witnesses/' | relative_url }}) at the centre of the Varginha incident did not report seeing a crashed spacecraft, wreckage, or a military recovery operation. Their original account was considerably narrower. On the afternoon of 20 January 1996, sisters Liliane and Valquíria Silva and their friend Kátia Xavier said they encountered an unusual crouching figure in the Jardim Andere district of Varginha, Brazil. Their frightened testimony became the starting point for one of the world's best-known UFO stories, but the wider [crash narrative]({{ 'crash-narrative/' | relative_url }}) developed later through additional claims made by other people. Understanding exactly what these three women reported—and what they did not report—is essential when assessing the evidential value of the case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://english.elpais.com/international/2026-01-20/the-et-of-varginha-captivates-brazil-30-years-after-sighting-dismissed-by-a-military-investigation.html?outputType=amp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: english.elpais.com">[EL PAÍS English]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">english.elpais.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on elpais.com.</span></span></span>

## The afternoon encounter in Jardim Andere

According to the witnesses, the encounter occurred at approximately 3:30 pm while they were walking through a vacant area in the Jardim Andere neighbourhood. They noticed what they believed was a living figure crouched close to a wall. Rather than approaching it, they stopped briefly, became frightened and ran away. Their account consistently describes a brief, unexpected encounter rather than a prolonged observation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://english.elpais.com/international/2026-01-20/the-et-of-varginha-captivates-brazil-30-years-after-sighting-dismissed-by-a-military-investigation.html?outputType=amp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: english.elpais.com">[EL PAÍS English]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">english.elpais.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on elpais.com.</span></span></span>

An important feature of the earliest testimony is that the witnesses did not describe any accompanying aerial object. They reported no explosion, impact, smoke, metallic debris or damaged craft nearby. Those elements belong to later claims made by other individuals and are separate from the original witness account. Even within UFO literature, the women's testimony is treated as an independent creature sighting rather than direct evidence of a crash.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufoevidence.org/cases/case498.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoevidence.org">[UFO Evidence]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoevidence.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Evidence The Varginha Incident (Brazil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO EvidenceThe Varginha Incident (Brazil) - Varginha, Brazil - January 20, 1996 - UFO EvidenceJanuary 20, 1996...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 20, 1996</span></span></span>

The women reportedly arrived home visibly distressed. Early retellings indicate that they initially struggled to explain what they had seen and described it in frightening or even supernatural terms before investigators suggested extraterrestrial interpretations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://english.elpais.com/international/2026-01-20/the-et-of-varginha-captivates-brazil-30-years-after-sighting-dismissed-by-a-military-investigation.html?outputType=amp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: english.elpais.com">[EL PAÍS English]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">english.elpais.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on elpais.com.</span></span></span>

## Which creature details appear in early reports

Although wording varies slightly between interviews conducted over the following years, several physical characteristics appear consistently in the earliest descriptions.

The witnesses described a figure that was:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* relatively short in stature;
* crouching close to the ground rather than standing upright;
* covered with dark brown or brownish skin that appeared shiny or oily;
* characterised by an unusually large head;
* distinguished by large, prominent red eyes;
* marked by three rounded protrusions or horn-like bumps on the head;
* thin in build with human-like arms and legs.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://english.elpais.com/international/2026-01-20/the-et-of-varginha-captivates-brazil-30-years-after-sighting-dismissed-by-a-military-investigation.html?outputType=amp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: english.elpais.com">[elpais.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">english.elpais.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on elpais.com.</span></span></span>

</div>

One recurring aspect of the testimony is that the figure appeared weak, frightened or distressed rather than threatening. Liliane Silva has repeatedly stated that her impression was that it seemed to be suffering or hiding. This emotional interpretation has remained relatively stable in later interviews, although it reflects the witness's perception rather than an independently verifiable fact.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufoevidence.com/case/varginha-aliens-1996/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoevidence.com">[UFO Evidence]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoevidence.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Evidence The Varginha Incident – UFOevidence</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO EvidenceThe Varginha Incident – UFOevidenceJanuary 20, 1996...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 20, 1996</span></span></span>

## What the witnesses consistently denied seeing

Just as important as what they described is what they never claimed to have witnessed.

Their original testimony does **not** include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* a flying saucer or other aircraft;
* a crash or explosion;
* metallic wreckage;
* military personnel capturing the figure;
* transportation of the figure;
* direct evidence connecting the creature to any spacecraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://english.elpais.com/international/2026-01-20/the-et-of-varginha-captivates-brazil-30-years-after-sighting-dismissed-by-a-military-investigation.html?outputType=amp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: english.elpais.com">[EL PAÍS English]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">english.elpais.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on elpais.com.</span></span></span>

</div>

These omissions matter because later versions of the Varginha story often merge numerous independent claims into a single continuous narrative. The three women's evidence supports only the claim that they believed they encountered an unusual figure during a brief afternoon sighting.

## How investigators interpreted the encounter

The encounter quickly attracted the attention of local UFO researchers, including lawyer and ufologist Ubirajara Rodrigues. According to later interviews with the witnesses, Rodrigues told them that they had not seen a demon or an ape but an extraterrestrial. While this did not create the encounter itself, it introduced a specific interpretive framework at a very early stage of the investigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://english.elpais.com/international/2026-01-20/the-et-of-varginha-captivates-brazil-30-years-after-sighting-dismissed-by-a-military-investigation.html?outputType=amp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: english.elpais.com">[EL PAÍS English]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">english.elpais.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on elpais.com.</span></span></span>

## The official alternative explanation

Brazilian military investigators later proposed that the witnesses had mistaken a local man—Luís Antônio de Paula, known locally by a nickname because of his speech difficulties—for an extraordinary creature. According to the inquiry, he was known for crouching, had an unusual appearance, and was outdoors despite rainy weather that afternoon. Investigators argued that poor visibility, fear and surprise could have contributed to a mistaken identification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://english.elpais.com/international/2026-01-20/the-et-of-varginha-captivates-brazil-30-years-after-sighting-dismissed-by-a-military-investigation.html?outputType=amp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: english.elpais.com">[EL PAÍS English]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">english.elpais.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on elpais.com.</span></span></span>

## What the testimony can and cannot establish

The three witnesses unquestionably reported an experience that they regarded as frightening and unusual. Their accounts are broadly consistent on several core features, including the location, the crouching posture, the brown body, the large red eyes and the prominent head. Those consistencies explain why the incident has remained central to discussions of the Varginha case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://english.elpais.com/international/2026-01-20/the-et-of-varginha-captivates-brazil-30-years-after-sighting-dismissed-by-a-military-investigation.html?outputType=amp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: english.elpais.com">[EL PAÍS English]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">english.elpais.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on elpais.com.</span></span></span>
